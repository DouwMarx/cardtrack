cardtrack briefing, 28 October 2026: Meta corrects a cyber score, Anthropic opens CVP managed services, Google reroutes Fairwind access

As of the 2026-10-28 snapshot, the four daily runs since the 25 October edition stored 54 new versions and added 10 documents. Eight of the 44 version pairs compared were substantive; five carry the classifier’s safety flag. Each diff below was read in full.

  • Meta corrected a cyber-capability score in the Muse Wonder model card (document, v815→v973). The ExploitGym result in the Meta Superintelligence Scaling Framework risk assessment went from 92 flags (21 on the intended vulnerability) to 20 intended flags. A note dated 23 October 2026 says the harness now tells the model which vulnerability to target, as the benchmark recommends, and that the “moderate or lower” risk threshold stands. The 71 captures through unintended vulnerabilities no longer appear. One of 17 changes in the corpus with a dated note.
  • Anthropic’s Cyber Verification Program (CVP) help page opened applications for managed services (document, v889→v990). Approved partners may now run less-restricted cyber models against customers’ systems under a new Cyber Defender Partner Addendum; customers receive only finished deliverables. Customer-facing products stay closed. A new line says approval alone does not activate access. The Google Cloud row now also requires accepting an Advanced AI Safety Addendum, and data sharing with Anthropic moves from per project to per model and location.
  • Google DeepMind’s Fairwind Program no longer points applicants to a form (document, v835→v1001). The access-request line changed from “fill in our form” to “contact your Cloud account team”, with a Google Cloud get-started link for non-customers. No note on the page.
  • Epoch AI revised the baseline behind its “5x the pre-Mythos record” headline on disclosed CVEs (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures; document, v154→v960). The pre-Mythos monthly record moved from about 440 to about 490 high- and critical-severity CVEs. New limitations say undisclosed findings are not counted (Anthropic claims over 10,000 from Project Glasswing) and that interest, not only capability, may drive the spike. Disclosed in the text, undated.

The fifth safety-flagged edit adds one related-work paragraph to Anthropic’s reward-seeker post (document, v474→v962); no finding changes.

Method, caveats and provenance
  • Source: a read-only snapshot of the cardtrack database taken 2026-10-28. Numbers trace to out/results.json, out/edition.json and the tables above.
  • Every consecutive version pair is diffed on extracted text and labelled by a large language model (LLM) sub-agent following a written taxonomy; 376 labels are high confidence, 89 medium, 8 low. 22 labels were overridden after checks against raw captures in earlier editions.
  • Change dates are detection dates. Content re-checks run on a rotation with a median gap of 6.0 days (90th percentile 13.0), so an edit can surface up to two weeks late.
  • Written by Claude Fable 5.1 without human review. Links go to the tracker’s document pages, where stored versions can be diffed.
