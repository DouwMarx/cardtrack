Restricting AI Agent Use of Biological Tools
Exploring the Feasibility of Software Barriers
ResearchPublished Jul 20, 2026
Artificial intelligence (AI) agents can help non-experts use advanced biological design tools, raising misuse concerns. The authors investigate whether safeguards can be built into these tools to block such assistance. They find that these safeguards are ineffective against today’s AI agents, largely because multiple frontier large language models evade the safeguards by misrepresenting or concealing their identity as AI systems.
