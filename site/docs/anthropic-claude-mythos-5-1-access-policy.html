Our most capable model class for cybersecurity and biology research.
Announcements

Availability and pricing
Claude Mythos 5.2 is available to vetted cyberdefenders and life scientists through our trusted access programs. Life sciences researchers can apply for advanced capabilities through the Life Sciences Verification Program. Security teams can apply for advanced cyber capabilities on Claude Mythos 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Mythos 5.1, and future models through the Cyber Verification Program.
Pricing for Claude Mythos 5.2 starts at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens.
Fast mode for Claude Mythos 5.2 is also available through our trusted access programs. It costs $16 per million input tokens and $80 per million output tokens.
Claude Security also now runs on Mythos 5.2.
Safeguards
Because Mythos 5.2 is highly capable for cybersecurity and biology research, it could be used both for good and for harm. We currently provide access to a set of vetted organizations through our verification programs.
We extensively test and evaluate our models to ensure they meet Anthropic’s standards for safety, security, and reliability. The accompanying system card covers safety results in depth.
Data retention
Using Mythos models requires 30-day data retention for safety monitoring by default. With Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS), automated safety monitoring is run on prompts and responses that are stored in a customer’s infrastructure, making it compatible with zero data retention (ZDR). Until EFS is available, eligible Cyber Verification Program customers who already have ZDR can keep it for Mythos 5.2.
Learn more about data retention. Learn more about Enterprise Frontier Safeguards.

Frequently asked questions
Making models as capable as Mythos Preview generally available comes with risks. The model’s capabilities in specific areas like cybersecurity and biology are advanced enough that they could be misused to create cyberattacks or dangerous weapons. To release Mythos-level capabilities more broadly, we developed Claude Fable models with safeguards that block or limit its performance in risky areas.
Security teams can apply for advanced cyber capabilities on Claude Mythos 5.2, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Mythos 5.1, and future models through the Cyber Verification Program. Life sciences researchers can apply for advanced capabilities through the Life Sciences Verification Program.
