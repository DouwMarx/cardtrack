Gemini 4 Carbon — Model Card

Model Cards are intended to provide essential information on Gemini models, including
known limitations, mitigation approaches, and safety performance. Model cards may be
updated from time to time; for example, to include updated evaluations as the model is
improved or revised. See the Google DeepMind site for a comprehensive list of model
cards.

Published: November 2026

Model Information

Description
Gemini 4 Carbon is our model with frontier performance in long-horizon agentic
workflows across complex knowledge work, engineering, cybersecurity and scientific
reasoning.

Model dependencies
Gemini 4 Carbon is based on Gemini 4 Argon.

Inputs
Text strings (e.g., a question, a prompt, document(s) to be summarized), images, audio,
and video files, with a combined input-output token context window of up to 1M.

Outputs
Text, with up to a 1M token input + output.

Intended Usage and Limitations

Benefit and Intended Usage
Gemini 4 Carbon is built for tasks that test the limits of a model’s intelligence, such as
complex, multi-step coding and debugging and open-ended scientific research and
discovery — using more thinking tokens than Gemini 4 Argon to explore hypotheses
thoroughly, reason deeply, and run extended verification loops, with configurable thinking
levels to balance quality, latency, and cost.

It is particularly well-suited for applications that require:

   ●​ high-precision knowledge work, artifact generation, and data analysis

   ●​ engineering tasks that require multi-agent collaboration

   ●​ strong visual design, spatial reasoning, and attention to environmental detail

   ●​ strong performance in reasoning across physics, math, and life sciences

   ●​ cybersecurity defense, vulnerability discovery, and patching (with specialized
      capabilities available to verified defenders via the Fairwind Program)

Known Limitations
Gemini 4 Carbon may exhibit some of the general limitations of foundation models, such
as hallucinations. In addition to this, we are continually working to improve jailbreak
resistance and have recently strengthened the mitigations across Frontier Safety. There
may also be occasional slowness or timeout issues, and at times the model may use more
tokens to maximize performance, especially at higher thinking levels. The knowledge
cutoff for Gemini 4 Carbon is March 2026, though in some domains the model’s
knowledge may be less up to date. For more information about known limitations, see the
Gemini 4 Argon model card.

Training and Development Evaluation Results
Results for some of the internal safety evaluations conducted during the development
phase are listed below. The evaluation results are for automated evaluations and not
human evaluation or red teaming. Scores are provided as an absolute percentage
increase or decrease in performance compared to the indicated model, as described
below.

Overall, Gemini 4 Carbon outperforms Gemini 4 Argon across multilingual and
multimodal content safety, while performing similarly on unjustified refusals and tone. We
mark improvements in bolded green.

See the Gemini 4 Carbon Safety & Security Report for details.

We continue to improve our internal evaluations, including refining automated evaluations

computed with improved evaluations and thus are not directly comparable with
performance results found in previous Gemini model cards.

We expect variation in our automated safety evaluations results, which is why we review
flagged content to check for egregious or dangerous material. Our manual review
confirmed losses were overwhelmingly either a) false positives or b) not egregious.

Human Red Teaming Results
We conduct manual red teaming by specialist teams who sit outside of the model
development team. High-level findings are fed back to the model team. For child safety
evaluations, Gemini 4 Carbon satisfied required launch thresholds, which were developed
by expert teams to protect children online and meet Google’s commitments to child
safety across our models and Google products. For content safety policies generally,
including child safety, we saw similar or improved safety performance compared to
Gemini 4 Argon. Additionally, the scope of red teaming covered potential issues outside
of our strict policies, compared performance to Gemini 4 Argon, and found no egregious
concerns.

Frontier Safety Assessment
For more information about our frontier safety assessment, see the Gemini 4 Carbon Safety &
Security Report.

Risks and Mitigations
Safety and security were built into Gemini 4 Carbon throughout the training and deployment
lifecycle, including pre-training data filtering, post-training alignment (supervised fine-tuning
and reinforcement learning), product- and system-level safety filtering, and usage monitoring.
In addition, we strengthened Frontier Safety safeguards for Chemical, Biological, Radiological,
and Nuclear (CBRN) and cybersecurity misuse, and provide specialized dual-use cybersecurity
capabilities to verified defenders through the Fairwind Program. Key ongoing focus areas
include resistance to adversarial jailbreaks and indirect prompt injections. For more information
about the risks and mitigations, see the Gemini 4 Carbon Safety & Security Report.
