Model Information

Description
Gemini 4 Neon is built for complex coding, analytical reasoning, and
production-grade enterprise workflows. While Carbon tackles your hardest,
long-horizon projects and Hydrogen handles fast everyday execution, Neon
balances depth and efficiency. It delivers frontier-level reasoning for complex,
high-frequency workflows faster and more cost efficiently.

Model dependencies
Gemini 4 Neon is adapted from Gemini 4 Carbon.

Inputs
Text strings (e.g., a question, a prompt, document(s) to be summarized), images, audio,
and video files, with a combined input-output token context window of up to 1M.

Outputs
1M unified context window with up to 64K default synchronous output tokens / up to 1M
via background long decode.

Intended Usage and Limitations

Benefit and Intended Usage
Gemini 4 Neon is built for complex coding, analytical reasoning, and production-grade
enterprise workflows, delivering frontier-level reasoning for complex, high-frequency
workflows faster and more cost efficiently.

It is particularly well-suited for applications that require:

   ●​ complex, tool-heavy tasks

   ●​ fast, disciplined execution on its own or as sub-agents orchestrated alongside
      frontier models

   ●​ supporting cyber defensive tasks, including secure code generation, penetration
      testing, vulnerability finding, validating, and patching

   ●​ iterative repository coding and debugging

   ●​ multimodal knowledge work

   ●​ ready-to-go writing, research, and everyday work

Known Limitations
Gemini 4 Neon may exhibit some general limitations such as hallucinations. In addition,
we are continually working to improve jailbreak resistance and have further strengthened
mitigations across Frontier Safety. The knowledge cutoff for Gemini 4 Neon is March
2026, though in some domains the model’s knowledge may be less up to date.

Frontier Safety Assessment
We evaluated Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon in line with the Frontier Safety Framework
(April 2026) and reported the results in the respective Safety and Security Reports (Argon,
Carbon). Gemini 4 Neon is part of the Gemini 4 series of models. We conducted a
Material Capability Change (MCC) assessment to understand whether Gemini 4 Neon has
meaningful new capabilities or material increases in performance relative to the most
recent model version subject to a critical capability assessment, which was Gemini 4
Argon. Gemini 4 Neon showed an increase in general capability under our MCC
assessment compared with Gemini 4 Argon, however this falls below our MCC threshold
and therefore a full suite of critical capability evaluations was not required in line with our
FSF. We carry forward our capability evaluation results and T/CCL determinations from

Risks and Mitigations
Safety and security were built into Gemini 4 Neon throughout the training and deployment
lifecycle, including pre-training data filtering, post-training alignment (supervised fine-tuning
and reinforcement learning), product- and system-level safety filtering, and usage monitoring.
In addition, we strengthened Frontier Safety safeguards for Chemical, Biological, Radiological,
and Nuclear (CBRN) and cybersecurity misuse. Key ongoing focus areas include resistance to
adversarial jailbreaks and indirect prompt injections. For more information about the risks and
mitigations, see the Gemini 4 Neon Safety & Security Report.
