Standing report, 21 November 2026 snapshot: cyber access policies at Anthropic, OpenAI and Google changed scope again without a change log, OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra card revised four times with notice, and a biology score understated 3.7-fold across five OpenAI documents

As of the 2026-11-21 snapshot, cardtrack holds 1,145 versions of 458 documents from 35 publishers. 119 version pairs are substantive publisher edits; 57 carry the safety flag set by the classifier, a language-model sub-agent that labels every diff. A-ids index the appendix; vN are stored version ids. Ranked by consequence for a safety reader:

  1. Cyber access gating: Anthropic’s Cyber Verification Program (CVP), OpenAI’s Daybreak, Google’s Fairwind (CVP article, A44 to A112; Claude Mythos page, A68 to A101; Daybreak overview, A40 to A116; Fairwind Program, A87, A113). These programmes decide who gets frontier models with cyber refusals reduced. The four pages hold 19 of the 119 edits and 18 of the 57 safety-flagged ones; none logs its edits, and the CVP article still reads “Updated October 6, 2026” after its 15 November rewrite (v1045→v1188). That rewrite named Mythos 5.2 (announced 10 November, A101), added seat caps and per-tier evidence requirements, kept the US-government review for Specialized Access, and deleted the section “Why might an organization not have access?”, Anthropic’s stated reasoning, which had promised “a more cautious approach” for customers serving “military, intelligence, or law enforcement”. OpenAI (v1027→v1237) opened GPT-5.6 Cyber to every Daybreak Red organisation, where it had needed separate approval, added GPT-6 Cyber under separate approval and GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, and restricted GPT-5.5-Cyber to existing holders.
  2. GPT-6 Astra System Card, OpenAI (document; A38, A50, A61, A99). Most-revised system card: four dated change-log revisions since the 3 September launch. The alignment section was renamed and hedged, a comparison plot removed, a HealthBench score corrected, and appendices added covering two further models and the always-on GPT-6 Astra agents OpenAI calls “dots”, including monitor-evasion results.
  3. GPT-5.5 protein-binding score, five OpenAI documents (GPT-5.5 card, v8→v387, A19; also A4, A11, A14, A22). The pass@4 row of the hard-negative protein-binding evaluation (pass@k: success within k attempts) carried the pass@1 value, 0.4%, from the 23 April card until a change log dated 19 August replaced it with 1.48%. The error understated capability; the corrected value stays below GPT-5.4-thinking’s 3.46%.
  4. Gemini 4 Argon Safety & Security Report, Google DeepMind (document, v834→v901, A77). The only edited system card or safety report in the corpus with no change log: published 13 October, changed by 21 October, no revision date. A footnote on “we avoid training directly against our monitors” now cross-references a disclosure that early reinforcement-learning runs let a reward-hacking penalty read the model’s thoughts. “We mitigated as if at CCL regardless” (Critical Capability Level) left the CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear) evaluation-awareness subsection.

Method, caveats and provenance
  • Source: a read-only snapshot of the cardtrack database taken 2026-11-22T02:00Z; last daily run 21 November. Numbers trace to out/results.json, out/standing_figs.json, the tables above and queries recorded in out/RUN_SUMMARY.md.
  • Every consecutive version pair is diffed on extracted text and labelled by the classifier (655 pairs; 537 high, 110 medium, 8 low confidence). 40 pairs are the tracker’s own URL migrations and are excluded. Substantive means content minor, content major or replacement. 34 labels carry verified overrides from raw-capture checks.
  • The safety flag is the classifier’s; the ranking rests on diffs read by the author (items 2 to 4 in earlier runs of this report). Change dates are detection dates and lag the edit by up to one re-check interval.
  • Outage runs are excluded from availability statistics. Hugging Face hosts 104 tracked documents; a 429 on re-check hides an edit for weeks.
  • Written by Claude Fable 5.1 without human review.
